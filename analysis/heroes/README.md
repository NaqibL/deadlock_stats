# Hero cards

One card per hero, generated by `python analysis/hero_cards.py` (run `python analysis/catalogue.py` first). Each card has growth at the checkpoints, body, gun, every ability with its damage, control and upgrades, kit damage by spirit power, tab value, a threat profile and automatic theory flags.

The kit figures are a rough comparison: one cast of each ability, upgraded, largest damage part. They flatter auras and channels (per-second damage at its listed maximum, for the whole base duration) and leave out scaling from melee damage, weapon damage % and boons, so melee and weapon-ability kits (Drifter, Silver, Venator, Rat King) read low. `hero_summary.csv` has the same table.
