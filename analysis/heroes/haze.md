## Abilities

- **Damage:** Damage 65 +2.2 × spirit power (+338% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Control:** sleep 2.75s; sleep 1s (tier 2 adds); move slow 40% (tier 3 adds).

### 2. Smoke Bomb: slot 2, cooldown 33 s

Fade out of sight, becoming invisible and gaining sprint speed. Attacking removes invisibility, but using items does not. Close enemies can see through your invisibility.

- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** heal 50 (tier 3).

- Tier 2 (2 AP): Charges +2; Charge Delay +7s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Bullet Lifesteal +50; Post Invis Buff Duration +5; Dispel On Use +1

### 3. Fixation: slot 3, no cooldown listed

Shooting a target increases your bullet damage on that target. Gain one stack per bullet hit, three if the hit is a headshot.

- **Damage:** Weapon Damage 0.2  [bullet, gun buff per bullet].

- Tier 3 (5 AP): Weapon Damage +0.11/stack and +0.00035 x weapon damage % scaling

### 4. Bullet Dance: ultimate, cooldown 165 s

Enter a flurry, firing your weapon at nearby enemies with perfect accuracy and added Bullet Damage.

- Tier 1 (1 AP): Weapon Damage +7
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Fire Rate +10%; Channel Move Speed +3
