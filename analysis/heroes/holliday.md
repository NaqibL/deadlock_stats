## Abilities

### 1. Powder Keg: slot 1, cooldown 28 s, 2 charges (7.5 s between)

Throw a barrel that explodes after a delay, dealing spirit damage, setting enemies on fire and applying knockup. The barrel can be detonated early by being shot, melee'd, or detonated by another barrel. The burn stacks, so overlapping barrels burn for their combined damage. Can be Alt-Cast to place the barrel in front of Holliday.

- **Damage:** Damage Per Second 13.33/s +0.175 × spirit power (+131% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, per second]; Impact Damage 40 +0.525 × spirit power (+131% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Control:** displacement 0.4s.
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -10s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Charges +1
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Impact Damage +40 and +0.25 x spirit power scaling; Damage Per Second +13.3333 and +0.0833 x spirit power scaling; Charge Delay -5s

### 2. Bounce Pad: slot 2, cooldown 41 s

Drop a bounce pad in the world that launches any hero. You explode on landing, dealing Damage to any nearby enemies. The explosion can only occur once per person per Bounce Pad.

- **Damage:** Stomp Damage 60 +0.372 × spirit power (+62% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -10s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Speed On Land +4; Speed On Land Duration +4
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Duration +0.7s

### 3. Crackshot: slot 3, cooldown 20 s

Headshots deal bonus Damage and applies a Fading Move Speed penalty. This effect can only occur when off cooldown. Cooldown is reduced by 50% on NPC hits. Crackshot ignores range damage fall-off and does not apply to objectives.

- **Damage:** Damage 55 +1.116 × spirit power (+203% per 100 spirit power) [weapon, flat].
- **Control:** move slow 20% (tier 1 adds).
- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** resist reduction -6% (tier 2).

- Tier 2 (2 AP): Damage +49.5; Bullet Resist Reduction -6%; Bullet Resist Reduction Duration +5
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Cooldown Per Headshot -6s; Cooldown Per Headshot NPC -3s

### 4. Spirit Lasso: ultimate, cooldown 130 s

Throw out your lasso, dealing spirit damage, pulling, and applying stun. Using a Bounce Pad extends the duration of Spirit Lasso.

- **Damage:** Damage 80 +0.93 × spirit power (+116% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Control:** stun (from description); displacement (from description).
