## Abilities

### 1. Essence Bomb: slot 1, cooldown 14 s

Sacrifice some of your health to launch a bomb that deals damage after a brief arm time. Self damage type is Spirit and can be mitigated. Bombs leave a toxic mess on the ground, dealing 30% of the original damage per second, for 6s.

- **Damage:** Damage 90 +1.35 × spirit power (+150% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -5s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Damage +50; Radius +2m
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Blood Spill DPSPercent +30; Toxic Mess Duration +6s

### 2. Life Drain: slot 2, cooldown 34 s

Create a tether that drains enemy health over time and heals you. Target must be in line of sight and within max range to drain. You can shoot and use other abilities during the drain, but your move speed is reduced. [Alt Cast] to give health to friendly Heroes.

- **Damage:** Damage Per Second 24/s +0.323 × spirit power (+134% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, per second].
- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** heal 100%.
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Damage Per Second +13
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Duration +2.5s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Charges +3; Charge Delay +0.1s; Damage Per Second +0.45 x spirit power scaling

### 3. Malice: slot 3, cooldown 6 s

Sacrifice some of your health to launch blood shards that apply a stack of Malice. Each stack slows the victim and increases the damage they take from you. The slow effect decreases over time.

- **Damage:** Damage 23 +0.558 × spirit power (+243% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Control:** move slow 12%.
- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** damage amp on target 7%; damage amp on target 8% (tier 3).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -3s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Damage +25.2; Blood Shards +4; Spread Angle Degrees +22
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Damage Amplification +8%

### 4. Soul Exchange: ultimate, cooldown 220 s

Swaps health levels with an enemy target. There is a minimum health percentage that enemies can be brought down to and a minimum amount of health received based on victims current health.

- **Control:** silence 3s (tier 2 adds).
- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** own resist 50% (tier 3).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -50s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Silence Duration +3s; Silence Radius +25m
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Self Buff Duration +8; Spirit Resist +50%; Fire Rate +40%; Spirit Power +60
