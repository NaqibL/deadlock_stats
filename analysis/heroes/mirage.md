## Abilities

### 1. Fire Scarabs: slot 1, cooldown 35 s, 2 charges (1 s between)

Infest an enemy with fire scarabs, stealing life from them and causing them to deal reduced damage.

- **Damage:** Damage Per Second 8/s +0.1 × spirit power (+125% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, per second].

### 2. Dust Devil: slot 2, cooldown 36 s

Transform yourself into a whirlwind that travels forward, damaging enemies, slowing their move speed and lifting them up in the air. After emerging from the tornado you gain bullet evasion.

- **Damage:** Damage 65 +0.3 × spirit power (+46% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Control:** move slow 24%; lift 0.3s.
- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** evasion 30%; evasion 4s; evasion 30% (tier 2).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Damage +60
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Cooldown -12s; Bullet Evasion Chance +30%
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Recast Window +6s; Damage +0.6 x spirit power scaling

### 3. Djinn's Mark: slot 3, cooldown 2.75 s

Active: Consume all Djinn Marks to deal their damage now. Passive: Your shots apply a Djinn Mark on the target. When the mark expires or is consumed, it deals spirit damage and the target is briefly revealed on the map. Each added mark doubles the current damage. There is a per target Cooldown for applying additional marks

- **Damage:** Base Damage 11 +0.35 × spirit power (+318% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat]; Damage Per Mark 2  [spirit, flat].

### 4. Traveler: ultimate, cooldown 140 s

Target a location on the minimap. After a brief wait, teleport to that location. Taking damage or being Stunned, Immobilized, put to Sleep or Movement Silenced during the wait period causes the teleport to be interrupted. After teleporting, gain a Barrier.

- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** barrier 0 (tier 3).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Move Speed +3m; Fire Rate +20%
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Cooldown -65s
