## Abilities

### 1. Leech Therapy: slot 1, cooldown 30 s

Throw a jar of leeches that attach to enemies, dealing spirit damage over time and eating away at the duration of enemy buffs. Harrow can pick up leeches after they finish feeding, healing her for the health drained and permanently granting regeneration and fire rate. 25% of your leech stacks are lost on death.

- **Damage:** Damage Per Second 23/s +0.19 × spirit power (+83% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, per second].
- **Other scaling:** Leech Crawl Range: 1.3 per boon; Health Regen Per Leech: 0.13 per boon.

- Tier 2 (2 AP): Cooldown -10s; Spirit Power Per Leech +4
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Max Leech Stacks +8; Damage Per Second +0.3 x spirit power scaling; Radius +1m

### 2. Wretched Vapors: slot 2, cooldown 40 s

Crack open a vial to release an ethereal vapor onto yourself or an ally that causes a rush, then a crash. Specter Rush: While it lasts, increases the speed of all actions and temporarily numbs all incoming damage creating looming specters. Specter Crash: When the vapors wear off, all actions are slower and any remaining specters deal their delayed damage plus extra damage. Incoming healing will prioritize removing specters first, preventing them from dealing damage.

- **Control:** move slow 20%.
- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** damage amp on target 30%; heal 20% (tier 2).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -15s; Cast Range +7m
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Duration +1s; Incoming Healing +20%; Health Regen Boost +20%
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Rush Speed +20%; Dispel Debuffs +1

### 3. Healing Needles: slot 3, no cooldown listed

Load your weapon with a full clip of syringes that heal allies. Dealing damage recovers charges, and each leech you have collected slowly restores them. Reloading spends a charge for a fresh clip. Unused syringes are refunded as a partial charge. Recast to put them away.

- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** heal 22; heal 15%; heal 16 (tier 2); heal 0 (tier 3).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Charges +1
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Stamina Per Shot +0.6; Heal Per Shot +16
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Heal Per Shot +0.4 x spirit power scaling; Buff Extension Per Shot +6

### 4. Lurking Terrors: ultimate, cooldown 200 s

Open your bag and reveal the horrors you carry inside, dealing spirit damage over time to enemies in front of you. Enemies looking at you build up terror and cannot use stamina or movement abilities. At max terror, they flee in fear away from you. Each leech you have collected adds fear duration. Enemies facing away from you take 50% damage unless feared.

- **Damage:** Damage Per Second 60/s +0.3 × spirit power (+50% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, per second].
- **Control:** move slow 30%; fear 2.2s; move slow 20% (tier 2 adds); fire rate slow 20% (tier 2 adds).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -50s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Move Speed Slow +20%; Fire Rate Slow +20%; Damage Per Second +30
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Damage Resist Pct While Channeling +50%; Channel Duration +3s; Damage Per Second +0.5 x spirit power scaling
