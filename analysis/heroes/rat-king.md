## Abilities

### 1. Scrap Grenade: slot 1, cooldown 26 s

Throw a grenade of scrap that detonates multiple times, dealing bullet damage and applying slow. The grenade can be meleed to knock it away. The final detonation has a longer fuse but increased detonation radius, deals increased bullet damage and applies a stronger slow.

- **Damage:** Detonation Damage 60 +0.6 × weapon damage % [weapon, flat].
- **Other scaling:** Detonation Damage: 0.6 per weapon damage %.
- **Control:** move slow 20%.
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -8s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Detonation Damage +60
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Detonations +1; Big Detonation Damage +275 and +1 x weapon damage % scaling; Big Detonation Radius +14m; Big Detonation Move Speed Slow +0.12 x spirit power scaling

### 2. Rat Swarm: slot 2, cooldown 24 s

Call forth a swarm of rats that leap onto enemies, dealing spirit damage over time and reducing their bullet resist with every bite. The more rats are attached, the faster they will bite the target. Enemies can have multiple rats attached, but can shake off a rat early by performing a ground or air dash.

- **Damage:** Damage per Bite 8 +0.08 × spirit power (+100% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Charges +1
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Rat Swarm Cols +2; Rat Swarm Rows +1; Rat Swarm Width +100; Debuff Duration +4s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Bullet Resist per Bite -1%; Damage per Bite +8

### 3. Royal Pestments: slot 3, cooldown 22 s

Adorn scrappy armor, gaining barrier based on your max health. As long as the armor holds, bullets have a chance to be deflected back towards the attacker. The barrier does not benefit from resists.

- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** barrier 15%; barrier 10% (tier 3).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Duration +4s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Bullet Return Chance +15%; Cooldown -8s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Max Health as Barrier +10%; Damage Cap +250

### 4. Rule, Ratannia!: ultimate, cooldown 180 s

Charge up and summon the banner of Ratannia, granting all nearby allies increased move speed, immunity from slows, and reduced incoming damage while near the banner. Slam the banner into the ground causing the sewers below to violently erupt, dealing bullet damage while applying knockup and slow to all enemies in the area. Rats will swarm out of the sewers, applying Rat Swarm to enemies they hit.

- **Damage:** Damage 200 +1.2 × weapon damage % [weapon, flat].
- **Other scaling:** Damage: 1.2 per weapon damage %.
- **Control:** move slow 40%; displacement (from description).
- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** own resist 15%; own resist 10% (tier 3).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Move Speed +2m; Cooldown -25s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Flag Duration +5s; Radius +8m
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Damage +150; Damage Reduction +10%
