## Abilities

### 1. Serrated Knives: slot 1, cooldown 16 s, 2 charges (2 s between)

Throw a knife that bleeds an enemy. Each additional hit adds a stack and refreshes the bleed duration, causing the bleed to increase per stack. Ultimate Unlock: While rage is full knives deal 4% of the target's current health as damage on impact and apply a slow to enemies they bleed.

- **Damage:** Bleed DPS Per Knife 10/s +0.13 × spirit power (+130% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, per second]; Current Health Damage 4% of health +0.015 × spirit power (+38% per 100 spirit power) [target health %, target health %].
- **Control:** move slow 28%.
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Bleed Duration +2s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Charges +2
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Bleed DPS Per Knife +12 and +0.07 x spirit power scaling; Current Health Damage +0.01 x spirit power scaling

### 2. Slice and Dice: slot 2, cooldown 16 s

Perform a dash forward, damaging enemies along the path. Hit Enemies have their spirit resist reduced. This debuff can stack. Ultimate Unlock: While rage is full an echo of Shiv retraces the dash path after a short delay, damaging enemies again.

- **Damage:** Impact Damage 75 +1.2 × spirit power (+160% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** own resist -6%; resist reduction -4% (tier 2).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -6s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Spirit Resist Reduction -4%; Time Window +2s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Impact Damage +50; Cooldown Reduced on Hero Hit +2s; Cooldown Reduced on Non-Hero Hit +1s; Max Cooldown Reduction +8

### 3. Bloodletting: slot 3, cooldown 25 s

Take only a portion of incoming damage immediately and defer the rest to be taken over time. Activate to clear a portion of the deferred damage. Ultimate Unlock: While rage is full the amount of damage deferred is increased.

- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** damage deferred 25%; damage deferred 6s; damage deferred 30%; damage deferred 15%; damage deferred 40% (tier 2); damage deferred 15% (tier 3).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -15s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Deferred Damage Cleared +40%
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Incoming Damage Deferred +15%

### 4. Killing Blow: ultimate, cooldown 145 s

Active: Leap forward, dealing spirit damage to the first enemy hero. If they are below the kill threshold, execute them instead. Finishing an enemy with Killing Blow lets you recast for 12s Passive: Damaging enemies fills you with rage. While at full rage, Shiv gains increased damage and unlocks special properties on his other abilities. Cooldown is increased by +30% if no hero is impacted.

- **Damage:** Damage 200  [spirit, flat].
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Move Speed +2m; Cast Range +6m
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Full Rage Damage Bonus +16%; Cooldown -25s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Enemy health threshold +10%
