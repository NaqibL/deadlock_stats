## Abilities

### 1. Vexing Bolt: slot 1, cooldown 24 s

Fire a bolt of magic that deals Damage, increasing as it travels. If you have an Assistant, they also cast Vexing Bolt at reduced damage. Press [Ability1] to redirect the bolt towards your crosshairs.

- **Damage:** Minimum Damage 60 +0.93 × spirit power (+155% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat]; Max Damage 120 +1.86 × spirit power (+155% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Control:** fire rate slow 25% (tier 1 adds).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Fire Rate Slow +25%; Debuff Duration +5s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Cooldown -13s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Max Damage +126; Assistant Damage +50%

### 2. Spectral Assistant: slot 2, cooldown 40 s

Summon an Assistant at the targeted location. The Assistant attacks whenever you fire your weapon, dealing Damage. While the Assistant is out, you can press [Ability2] to swap positions with your Assistant. Casting Spectral Assistant also reloads your weapon.

- **Damage:** Damage 15 +0.36 × spirit power (+240% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -10s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Duration +7s; Cast Range +5m; Max Leash Radius +5m
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Fire Rate +60%; Damage +12.6

### 3. Rabbit Hex: slot 3, cooldown 26 s

Hex a target area and transform all enemies into a Rabbit for a limited duration. Rabbits are small and move faster, but take increased Damage and are unable to perform most actions. Hex does not interrupt abilities.

- **Control:** hex 2s; hex 1s (tier 2 adds).
- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** damage amp on target 15%; damage amp on target 7% (tier 3).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -10s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Hex Duration +1s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Radius +2m; Damage Amp +7%

### 4. Audience Participation: ultimate, cooldown 85 s

Copy the Ultimate of an enemy hero for a limited time. Reactivating the ability will use the Copied Ultimate instead. Copies will inherit this ability's upgrade points. Audience Participation will also copy the cooldown.

- Tier 1 (1 AP): Upgrade the Copy.
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Upgrade the Copy AGAIN!
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Upgrade the Copy ONCE AGAIN!
