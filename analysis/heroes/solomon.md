## Abilities

### 1. Setup Bishop: slot 1, cooldown 12 s

Create a bishop that remains in place indefinitely. Move ([ADS]): Moves the bishop, applying a fading slow and dealing spirit damage to enemies it passes through. After a delay, activates diagonal squares that deal additional spirit damage when stepped on. Re-activate to recall all bishops, ending the ability.

- **Damage:** Damage 25 +0.85 × spirit power (+340% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat]; Impact Damage 70 +2 × spirit power (+286% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Other scaling:** Move Distance: 0.23 per boon.

- Tier 2 (2 AP): Max Deployed Pieces +1; Recast Window +8s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Spirit Resistance per Stack -5%; Max Stacks +5; Stack Duration +10s; Square Range +1

### 2. Kingside Knight: slot 2, cooldown 25 s

Promote an ally to be a knight, granting bonus stamina and barrier for a duration. If self-cast you develop yourself as king instead, granting double the barrier but no stamina. Move ([ADS]): Moves the piece, making it invulnerable during the duration before landing. If self-cast, re-activate the ability to move instead.

- **Other scaling:** Move Distance: 0.23 per boon; King Move Distance: 0.14 per boon.
- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** barrier 80 (tier 2).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -8s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Stamina +1; Barrier +80
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Max Deployed Pieces +1; Bonus Target Radius +20; Remove Debuffs +1

### 3. Rook Battery: slot 3, cooldown 33 s

Release a pair of rooks that travel forward, dealing spirit damage and applying immobilize. If the rooks hit a wall, they reflect back in the opposite direction. Re-Activate: Split the rooks perpendicularly.

- **Damage:** Damage 50 +0.9 × spirit power (+180% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Control:** immobilize 1s; fire rate slow 25% (tier 1 adds); immobilize 0.5s (tier 3 adds).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Fire Rate Slow +25%; Debuff Duration +5s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Current Health +5%
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Cooldown -10s; Immobilize Duration +0.5s; Wall Reflects +1

### 4. Develop Queen: ultimate, cooldown 240 s

Summon a queen that crashes down, dealing spirit damage in an area. After a delay, the queen releases squares in straight lines that deal spirit damage again and apply stun. Heroes hit that are low health are checkmated, executing them. Move ([ADS]): The queen leaps to the target location, dealing the same effects again. Re-activate to recall the queen early, ending the ability.

- **Damage:** Damage 120 +1.6 × spirit power (+133% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Other scaling:** Checkmate Threshold: 5 per boon; Move Distance: 0.23 per boon.
- **Control:** stun 1.25s; move slow 15%; move slow 15% (tier 2 adds).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -40s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Checkmate Threshold +150; Move Speed Slow +15%
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Square Range +1; Additional Moves +2
