## Abilities

### 1. Call Bell: slot 1, cooldown 18 s

Throw out a call bell that deals spirit damage on impact. After a short delay it explodes, dealing additional spirit damage and causing affected enemies to suffer reduced weapon accuracy and movement slow. Can be shot to detonate early.

- **Damage:** Impact Damage 40 +0.7 × spirit power (+175% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat]; Explosion Damage 55 +1.2 × spirit power (+218% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Control:** move slow 28%; accuracy / vision -40%.
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Charges +1
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Impact Damage +30; Explosion Damage +40
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Projectile Fuse +26; Radius +4.5m; Impact Damage +0.35 x spirit power scaling; Explosion Damage +0.35 x spirit power scaling

### 2. Doorway: slot 2, cooldown 45 s

Place two connected doors in the world. Players and most projectiles entering one door will exit out through the other. The doors will close at the end of the duration. The two doors must be placed within the doorway distance of each other.

- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** barrier 250 (tier 2); barrier 12s (tier 2).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Duration +20s
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Barrier +250 and +1.5 x spirit power scaling; Barrier Duration +12s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Doorway Distance +40m and +0.15 x spirit power scaling; Cast Range +30m

### 3. Luggage Cart: slot 3, cooldown 30 s

Send out a Luggage Cart that deals spirit damage and pulls enemy heroes along its path. Can be alt cast with [Alt Cast] to target friendly heroes instead. If the cart collides with a wall while dragging an enemy, it will deal bonus damage to the targets.

- **Damage:** Cart Damage 60 +0.75 × spirit power (+125% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Control:** displacement (from description); stun 1.25s (tier 3 adds).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cart Damage +80
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Cast Range +25m; Wall Impact Damage +90 and +0.6 x spirit power scaling
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Stun Duration +1.25s; Cooldown -18s

### 4. Hotel Guest: ultimate, cooldown 140 s

Send the target's physical body to be a guest at the Baroness Hotel. The guest is to promptly make their way to the exit elevator, where they'll be sent back to their original position. A stay at the Baroness Hotel is paid for in spirit damage. Failure to check-out on time costs additional spirit damage.

- **Damage:** Damage 75 +1 × spirit power (+133% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat]; Damage 125 +1.5 × spirit power (+120% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Control:** stun 1.5 (tier 2 adds).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Cooldown -20s; Stamina Drain per Second +1
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Damage +150; Late Checkout Damage +150; Late Checkout Stun +1.5
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Unstoppable While Hotel Occupied +1; Late Checkout Cooldown +15
