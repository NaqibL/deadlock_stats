## Abilities

### 1. Splatter: slot 1, cooldown 26 s

Throw a ball of goo that deals damage and leaves puddles of goo behind that apply movement slow to enemies in the radius. Multiple hits to the same target deal less damage. Goo puddles linger on the ground and apply a movement slow to enemies walking on them. You and your allies slide faster on goo puddles.

- **Damage:** Damage 55 +0.7 × spirit power (+127% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
- **Control:** move slow 28%.
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Damage +45; Radius +1.5m
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Cooldown -14s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Bounces +1; Damage +0.9 x spirit power scaling

### 2. The Cube: slot 2, cooldown 42 s

Encase the target in a cube of restorative goo that protects from damage, and increases health regen. Target is unable to take any new actions while cubed. Can be used on self. Press [Mantle] to escape early. After exiting the cube, the target has increased move speed and stamina recovery for a short duration.

- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** heal 40; heal 25 (tier 2).
- Tier 1 (1 AP): Move Speed +2.5m; Stamina Recovery +30%; Post Cube Buff +1
- Tier 2 (2 AP): Health Regen +25; Duration +1s
- Tier 3 (5 AP): Cooldown -20s; Purge Debuffs +1

### 3. Puddle Punch: slot 3, cooldown 24 s

Materialize a fist in the world that punches everyone in the area, applying knockup. Punching an enemy deals melee damage and applies slow. Enemies can Parry this attack. You and your allies have increased Air Control.

- **Damage:** Damage 20  [melee, flat].
- **Other scaling:** Damage: 0.6 per light melee damage.
- **Control:** move slow 24%; displacement 0.6s.
- **Defence, sustain, debuffs:** heal 60 (tier 2).

- Tier 3 (5 AP): Cooldown -14s; Use Heavy Melee +1; Damage +40 and +0.6 x heavy melee damage scaling; Damage -40

### 4. Goo Ball: ultimate, cooldown 150 s

Morph into a large goo ball that deals damage and stuns enemies on impact. The ball grants large amounts of Bullet and Spirit resist, bounces off walls and can double jump.

- **Damage:** Damage 110 +1 × spirit power (+91% per 100 spirit power) [spirit, flat].
