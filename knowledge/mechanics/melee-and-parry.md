## Patch history (101 lines, newest first)

**2026-09-16** ([forum post](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1844115010490072))

- Using parry now pauses reloading
- Parrying while doing a regular dash will now cancel the dash (and momentum) and parry on the spot
- Dashes and light melee's no longer pause your gun's cycle time (this is a general change that affects high-cycle time weapons the most like Silver or Abrams)

**2026-07-09** ([forum post](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1836506165584438))

- Rift Troopers now have Melee Resistance (25%)

**2026-06-30** ([forum post](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1836506165563227))

- Urn cannot be manually dropped. It gets dropped when you get hit with a light or heavy melee, are stunned or are killed. It stays where it drops and does not walk back home.
- Urn runner can no longer parry

**2026-06-04** ([forum post](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1834602721188293))

- Delivering the urn now starts a king of the hill style capture point, rather than using melee to flip the urn back and forth.

**2026-05-28** ([forum post](https://forums.playdeadlock.com/threads/05-22-2026-update.135477/post-261657))

- Parrying a [[trooper]] no longer resets your parry cooldown (however it won't end your parry early like a hero hit does)
- Parrying a trooper now stuns the trooper

**2026-05-22** ([forum post](https://forums.playdeadlock.com/threads/05-22-2026-update.135477/))

- Fixed Parry FX not always matching state

- While in the Depositing phase, the enemy team can Heavy Melee the urn to have their team claim it and cause it to switch sides. This adds +1.25s to that team's timer.

**2026-04-10** ([forum post](https://forums.playdeadlock.com/threads/04-10-2026-update.125825/))

- Parrying is now allowed while ground dashing in order to bring it in-line with air dashing
- Parry anti mash protection previously would last for 0.25s after the parry success (so you couldn't parry again for that duration). It is now for 0.25s only after the initial parry input is provided, irrespective of when the parry success happens (this means there is less delay before you can do a back to back parry when parry success is done later in the duration)
